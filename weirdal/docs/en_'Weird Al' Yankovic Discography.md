# "Weird Al" Yankovic Discography

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export a
config file.

## What does randomization do in this implementation?

Listening to Weird Al's major albums are locations in this implementation, which are controlled by various options in the YAML file, including which albums are enabled.
Extra YAML options also exist for enabling the inclusion of *Medium Rarities* (the bonus compilation included in the 2017 *Squeeze Box* box set) and a selection of miscellaneous singles ("You're Pitiful", "The Hamilton Polka", "Now You Know", and "Polkamania!").

## What is the goal of "Weird Al" Yankovic Discography?

The goal is to listen to all songs in all enabled albums. The client used for this implementation keeps track of your progress internally, and the goal is reached upon listening to all albums completely.

This APWorld is built off of bennydreamly's [Taylor Swift Discography](https://github.com/benny-dreamly/Archipelago/releases/tag/ts-1.2.1) APWorld, used with their explicit permission.
